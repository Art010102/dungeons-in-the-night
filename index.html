The three halls

Ember Halls

The first fire, the first blood, the first dragon. Learn the stone. Spend the fireball like you mean it.

Forked Dark

A split path and worse footing. The same hunger. Clear the first hall or this one stays shut.

Night's Crown

The last stone, the last coin, the last beast on the gate. The night does not wait.

How it plays

Landscape the moment a hall opens. Pad on the left. Strike, jump, and spell on the right. One cell of mana. One fireball. Eight damage. Behind the dragon sits a flask — kill the beast, or kite it, steal the flask, and burn it twice.

Sword & jump

Close in. Cut. Land on heads. One-way ledges. The floor is solid relief stone to the bottom of the world.

Coins & heal

Three spinning coins in each hall. A heal in the stone. Words rise off the floor: HP restored. Mana restored.

The dragon

Two and a half times your height. It does not fly. Come close and it runs you down. The banner is behind it.
