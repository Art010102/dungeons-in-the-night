Privacy PolicyDungeons in the Night

Effective 22 September 2026 · Package com.dungeonsnight.game · Version 1.0.0

This Policy is for the Android game Dungeons in the Night (the “Game”). It is written for the thing the Game actually does: a knight, three halls, slimes, bats, a dragon, and a save file that never leaves the phone.

1. Who runs this

The Game is published under the package name com.dungeonsnight.game. Contact for this Policy: aleksandr.arter@gmail.com.

2. The short version

We do not collect personal data. The Game has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporter, and no internet permission. It cannot phone home. Play on the subway. Play in a cave. The halls stay on the device.

3. What stays on your phone

Progress is written only to Android local storage (SharedPreferences), in a private file named dungeons_in_the_night. That file holds:

  • XP — experience earned in the halls
  • Unlocked hall — which of the three levels you may enter
  • Coin count — coins taken from the dark
  • Volume — the dungeon music slider
  • Language — English, Deutsch, Français, 中文, or 日本語

No name, email, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, or payment data is written there. Uninstalling the Game deletes this file.

4. Permissions

The Game requests no dangerous permissions and no INTERNET permission. Landscape orientation, sound, and on-screen controls run on the device alone.

5. Android backup

Android may, if you turned on device backup in system settings, copy app data into your Google account. That is the operating system, not the Game. We do not receive that copy. Turn backup off, or clear the app’s storage, if you do not want a local save to follow the phone.

6. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect data from children, because it does not collect data from anyone. It is a fantasy action game with combat against slimes, bats, and a dragon. Parents decide whether that tone fits.

7. Third parties

No ad network. No analytics SDK. No social login. No cloud save of ours. Google Play itself is Google’s store; their rules apply when you download or update the Game from Play. This Policy covers the Game after it is installed on the device.

8. Your choices

  • Change language and volume in Settings. That stays on the device.
  • Clear the app’s storage in Android settings to reset XP, halls, and coins.
  • Uninstall the Game to remove it and its local save.

9. Changes

If the Game ever starts talking to a server — it does not, today — this page will be updated before that ships, and the date above will move. The current text matches version 1.0.0.

10. Contact

Questions about this Policy: aleksandr.arter@gmail.com.
